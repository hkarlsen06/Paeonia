# Implementation Map

This is the first stop when an agent needs to find the current owner of a Paeonia behavior. Keep this file factual and source-oriented; durable rules still belong in `AGENTS.md`.

## Launch, Root State, And Navigation

- `ios/PaeoniaApp/App/RootView.swift`: root body, top banner mounting, and the cold-launch intro (`LaunchExperienceView`), which stays up until the intro signals it has fully unmasked the app (not just when `state` leaves `.launching`). `RootView+Screens.swift` maps each `AppState` to one screen and puts profile setup, the paywall, pairing, joining and the account notices in `PaeoniaScreenFrame` (`Shared/DesignSystem/Components/PaeoniaScreenFrame.swift`), so only their content crossfades. The logo shows on the welcome screen only. `PaeoniaScreenScroll` in the same file sets a screen's content a little above the middle of the space over its bottom bar and scrolls when it does not fit; `RootView+Actions.swift` forwards user actions and reacts to session, state, and invite-code changes (an invite link opened while paired is dropped with a banner); `RootView+System.swift` owns deep links, widget sync, push registration, and the foreground pass (one pass at a time); `RootView+PermissionPrimers.swift` asks for nothing when the app opens. The primers wait until the person has answered a question or sent a drawing (`MainTabActions.sentSomethingToPartner`, called when the answer cover or the drawing screen closes). The notification primer comes first, and at most one primer shows per visit. The foreground pass refreshes location next to the sync, so content never waits on GPS.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/App/PaeoniaApp.swift`: builds `RootViewModel` and `LocationMapViewModel` once (after Clerk is configured) and gives the root view model a Clerk session check, so a session that ends while the app runs is noticed.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/Auth/LaunchExperienceView.swift`: the cold-launch intro that continues the system launch screen. The composed petal mark holds only until `contentReady`. Then the two petal halves (`PaeoniaMarkPetalLeft`/`PaeoniaMarkPetalRight`, split from `PaeoniaMark` on the same viewBox) move apart while a circular hole opens from the center. The hole masks the whole overlay, so it cuts away the petals and the plum canvas together, then grows past the corners to unmask the first app surface. The petals ease out and the hole eases in, so the hole catches them about 70 ms in and the reveal finishes after 350 ms. The overlay stops taking touches when the reveal starts. Reduce Motion fades the overlay instead. No wordmark reveal.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/Auth/LaunchReveal.swift`: the coordination glue between the intro and the surface it uncovers — `EnvironmentValues.launchContentRevealed` (cues eligible content as the intro exits) and `EnvironmentValues.launchSidesUncovered` (true once the opening hole reaches the screen's sides), and `EnvironmentValues.launchIntroComplete` (true once the intro fully finishes), all set on the routed content in `RootView` and defaulting `true` outside the cold launch, plus reusable entrance modifiers for surfaces that still use a staggered reveal. `PairedHomeView` keeps its Home cards stationary on cold launch so the opening petals remain the focal motion; `CoupleMapCard`'s first flame sweep waits on `launchSidesUncovered` so it plays as the screen opens, not hidden behind the overlay.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/Auth/AuthBaselineViews.swift`: `AuthLaunchingView`, the quiet, copy-free loading surface shown under the cold-launch intro. Also the deleting-account and relationship-ended screens. Scenarios `onboarding-usage`, `relationship-ended`, `account-closed` and `deleting-account` open the single-purpose screens after sign-in in their frame.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/Auth/Onboarding/AuthOnboardingView.swift`: profile setup after the first sign-in (a name and a cropped photo, both required; an imported Google photo counts). `AuthOnboardingPhotoField.swift` is the photo picker: an empty dashed circle that asks for a photo, and after Continue without one a shake, a warm glowing ring and a callout that opens the picker (`AuthOnboardingPhotoNudge` counts the taps). A missing name only brings up the keyboard once there is a photo. Scrolls, keeps Continue above the keyboard with a loading state, and puts sign out and delete account in quiet links under it. The start date is asked before sign-in (`Welcome/WelcomeStartDateStep.swift`).
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/Auth/Welcome/`: the signed-out flow. `WelcomeFlowModel.swift` is the state machine (`WelcomeStep`: welcome, start date, drawing, sign-in with a `WelcomeSignInMode` of start, joining or returning), and `WelcomeFlowView.swift` crossfades the steps. Only the welcome screen shows the logo. Every later step sits in `PaeoniaScreenFrame`, the same frame as the screens after sign-in, with Back at the top level with the drawing screen's back button. `WelcomeView.swift` is the first screen: the `PaeoniaWordmark`, `WelcomeWidgetDoodle.swift` (a Home Screen drawing widget tile where a partner's handwritten note writes itself in once on launch, stroke by stroke with a tapered pen; the strokes are in `WelcomeDoodleInk.swift`, "miss you" in English and "savner deg" in Norwegian; touching the canvas clears the note and lets the person draw with the app's pen in `WelcomeFingerDoodle.swift`. Start our space carries that doodle to the first drawing: it waits in `WelcomeFlowStore` as `carriedDoodle` and `WidgetDrawingViewModel.addStrokesOnceLoaded` puts it on the draft once the canvas has loaded, one undo step per stroke. Every other way out drops it), the headline "Draw something. It shows up on their Home Screen.", then "Start our space", "I was invited" and "I already have an account". "I was invited" opens `PairingCodeEntrySheet`. When the clipboard holds a link, which the `/join` web page copies before sending a partner to the App Store, that row becomes a system Paste button. A pasted join link saves its code, and anything else opens the code sheet. A saved code (from there, a link, or the sign-in step's Change code) moves on to sign-in for joining, and the join screen opens for it after profile setup. The start path is `WelcomeStartDateStep.swift` (the date and `RelationshipDaysReveal`; the date waits in `WelcomeFlowStore.swift` and goes into the outbox as `FirstDayStartedOnQueue`, kind `setFirstDayStartedOn`, when `RootView+Actions.sessionChanged` sees a session), then `WelcomeDrawingStep.swift`: the real `WidgetDrawingView` with no owner, couple or live relay and without its share button or "Not sent yet" pill, and `WelcomeDrawingUploader.swift`, whose send only marks the saved drawing `isFirstDay`. Before sign-in the drawing and its marker live in `WelcomeDrawingStorage.swift`, a canvas in `Application Support/WelcomeDrawing` and a marker under its own key, apart from the widget's, so another account's data left on the phone (`RootDeviceOwnerStore`) never shows on the canvas and clearing it at sign-in (`RootViewModel.clearDataFromAnotherAccount`) never reaches the drawing. The drawing then lands on `WidgetHomeScreenMock`. After that clearing, `WelcomeFlowModel.sessionStarted` moves it to `WidgetCanvasService.shared` with the `isFirstDay` marker in `WidgetPendingUploadStore.shared`, and the invite screen sends it (`InviterFirstDayModel.load`). Choosing to join or to sign in to an existing account drops it. `WelcomeSignInStep.swift` has `WelcomeSignInPicture` (the waiting first drawing on the Home widget card's tile, rendered by `WelcomeFlowModel.waitingDrawingImage`, or else `WelcomeHero`, the pink and lavender peonies from the `Welcome` asset folder, sized to the free height), the headline per mode, white Continue with Apple, Continue with Google, the saved-code row for joining, and in-app privacy, terms and support links. Only the tapped provider shows a spinner (`RootViewModel.signingInWith`). Someone who signed in on this phone before opens at sign-in for returning, with Back to welcome. Deleting the account starts over at welcome. `WelcomeFunnelRecorder.swift` counts how many installs reach each step (see `backend/convex/onboarding.ts`): once per step per install, the step name only, no auth header, not PostHog. Scenarios `welcome`, `sign-in`, `sign-in-invite`, `welcome-start-date`, and `local-flow-fresh` for the whole journey.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/App/RootViewModel.swift`: root auth/access/pairing state. `RootRoute.swift` is the route enum; while access resolves after sign-in or profile setup the route holds that screen (with its button spinner) instead of showing an empty one. The selected tab resets to Home whenever the state leaves `.paired`. `RootViewModel+Account.swift` owns sign-in, sign-out, deletion (cancelling the Apple re-sign-in sheet keeps the account), profile saves (success is reported once the profile is saved; sync follows), and sessions that end on their own (back to sign-in with a banner, local data kept). `RootDeviceOwnerStore.swift` remembers whose data is on the phone, so signing in as someone else clears it like a sign-out. Installs from before the marker use the widget's saved identity instead. `RootViewModel+Launch.swift` is the cold launch: `PaeoniaApp.init` calls `beginLaunch()`, which starts the session restore (`ClerkAuthService.restoreSession`, one `identity:launch` call that returns the profile and the access snapshot) and reads the saved state before the scene exists, and `start()` awaits them. `RootViewModel+AccessResolution.swift` resolves access. `showSavedLaunchPreview()` opens Home on the first frame from the saved session and a saved paired snapshot (only when the phone holds this account's data and no privacy purge is pending), without committing the route, purging or celebrating, and the launch result settles it. Other saved states wait for the server. Without a launch result, a slow cold launch shows a saved paired snapshot after one second. `RootViewModel+Sync.swift` starts sync and stops if the session ended during startup. `RootViewModel+Refresh.swift` re-checks access after a purchase, a renewal, or a pairing change without restoring the session, so a failed check never signs anyone out. `RootViewModel+State.swift` holds the read-only state the views use. `RootPairingCelebrationState.swift` decides when the celebration and the first day sequence after it show. Tests in `ios/PaeoniaAppTests/RootViewModelFlowTests.swift`, `RootViewModelLaunchTests.swift` and `RootSessionLifecycleTests.swift`.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/AppLock/`: the optional Face ID, Touch ID or passcode lock. It is off by default, stored on the phone only (`UserDefaultsAppLockStore`, keys `paeonia.appLock.*`), and cleared by the sign-out and account deletion purge (`LocalPrivacyPurgeService`, `AppLockModel.shared.reset()`). `AppLockModel` decides the state. A cold launch starts locked. A trip to the background locks when the time away reaches the chosen grace (`AppLockPolicy`, Right away, 1 minute by default, or 15 minutes; measured with `ContinuousClock`). An app that only became inactive (Control Center, a system sheet, the Face ID prompt) never locks, but shows the cover as a shield so the app switcher snapshot stays blank. `AppLockCoverController` puts `AppLockCoverView` in its own `UIWindow` above everything, including sheets, and feeds the model the `UIScene` activate, deactivate and background notifications synchronously. The cover continues the launch screen (same petal mark, same place) and shows the Unlock button only when locked. The prompt opens by itself on launch and on return from the background, and never again after a cancel. `LocalAuthenticationAppLockAuthenticator` uses `.deviceOwnerAuthentication`, so the phone's passcode is always the fallback. A phone that lost its passcode turns the lock off and shows a banner, so nobody is locked out, and the lock cannot be turned on without a passcode. Turning it on or off needs a successful authentication. `AppLockRootModifier` is mounted on the routed content in `RootView+Screens.swift` (installs the cover, provides `AppLockModel` in the environment, shows the banner). While locked, `RootView+System.swift` holds deep links and notification taps (`isHeldByAppLock`, `deepLinkForTabs`) and routes them after the unlock. The lock does not cover widgets, notification actions or the notification extension, and the settings footer says so. `AppLockSettingsView.swift` holds the Settings screen and its row (the App section, hidden when the environment has no model, as in developer flows). The row and button name the right biometry (`AppLockBiometry.name`). `NSFaceIDUsageDescription` is in `Info.plist` and the localized `InfoPlist.xcstrings`. Tests in `ios/PaeoniaAppTests/AppLockModelTests.swift`.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/App/RootPrivacyLifecycle.swift`: cold-launch/foreground draining and bounded recovery for durable local privacy-purge retries; blocks a new paired route and sync startup until an older owner-scoped purge has completed.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/App/MainTabView.swift` and `ios/PaeoniaApp/App/MainTab.swift`: tab layout and high-level feature entry points. `MainTabIdentity` and `MainTabActions` carry the root's data and callbacks; `MainTabModels` builds the tab view models once. The loads that Home's first frame does not need (topics, games, favorites, reactions, chat summaries) wait for `launchIntroComplete` through `introGatedLoadKey`. `MainTabView+Flows.swift` owns the daily answer covers, streak sheets, and deep links (each link checks it is still current after its refresh). The tabs are Home, Questions, Games, Memories, Us. Each tab icon is an SF Symbol.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/App/DeveloperScenarios/`, `ios/PaeoniaApp/App/RootFeatureDependencies.swift`, and the shared `Scenarios` Xcode scheme: Debug-only deterministic state launcher plus interactive local flows through the production root. It replaces sign-in, StoreKit, Convex, APNs, sync, and persistence boundaries with in-memory dependencies. Use `PAEONIA_SCENARIO=<id>` or `-paeonia-scenario <id>` for direct UI-test launches. The extension contract is in `docs/developer-scenario-harness.md`.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/App/RootNoticeLocalization.swift`: root-level user-facing notice copy.
- `ios/PaeoniaAppTests/AccessRouteResolverTests.swift`: access route behavior around launch and relationship access.

When a launch-time network call is cancelled with `NSURLErrorCancelled` / `URLError.cancelled` shortly after render, inspect `.task(id:)` keys before hiding the error. The known Daily Challenge case is documented in `AGENTS.md`.

## Daily Challenge

- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Challenge/DailyChallengeView.swift`: the Questions tab. From the top: the hero card, today's list, and the earlier days. The hero is `DailyPromptCard` until `homeCardState.kind == .complete`, then `DailyChallengeDoneCard`. Both are the `DailyFlowZoom.questions` zoom source and open the answer flow. The screen owns the earlier-days `DailyChallengeHistoryViewModel`, built once the signed-in user is known (`makeHistoryViewModel()`). It loads on appear when nothing has loaded yet, on pull to refresh, and when the couple day changes. A reload that fails over existing days raises a banner. The screen also owns the chat navigation (`openChat` and `chatQuestion`).
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Challenge/DailyChallengeDoneCard.swift`: the one-row card that replaces the big prompt card on the Questions tab once the user has answered today's question. It says the user is done, plus one line about what happens next (`DailyChallengeCardState.completeFollowUp`: an earlier question the partner answered waits below, waiting for the partner, or both answered). Earlier questions waiting for the user show as locked rows inside it (`LockedPartnerQuestionRows`). Home keeps the full `DailyPromptCard`.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Challenge/DailyChallengeViewModel.swift`: the view model's stored state and init, plus `configure`, `reload`, `apply`, the live update wiring, day-end handling, `startToday`, streak refresh, notices, the home card state, and reveal coordination (`awaitAnswerReveal`). The answering, drafts, sending, and chat code lives in extensions in the same folder, so state shared across files is internal rather than private. `apply` drops the active day once its `endsAt` has passed (`DailyChallengeSnapshot.endingCurrentDay(ifEndedBy:)`, with an injected `now` clock), and a day-end task re-applies that and reloads at the end of the day, so an ended day never shows as today, even offline. `submitAnswer` refuses an answer after its day ended unless the partner already answered (`Notice.dayEnded`, draft kept) and any text over `DailyAnswerDraft.maxTextLength` (2,000 code points, the backend limit), because the server would reject both and the outbox would retry forever. `isSending` turns false as soon as the snapshot carries the sent answer. `refreshSendingState` runs on every live apply and on `.pendingOperations` changes, and skips a store read that started before a newer local send (`sendingGeneration`). On outbox changes it also picks up answers the outbox put back into the drafts and raises `Notice.submitFailed`. Skip swaps today's question for both partners and is offered only while neither has answered it (`DailyChallengeQuestion.canBeSkipped`). It is also gated on `hasDraftContent` (anything typed or staged), not on whether the draft can be sent. Photos are compressed off the main actor in `stagePhoto` with a per-question token, so a removal, skip, or newer pick wins, and Send waits for the photo. `shuffle` also deletes the skipped question's staged media. `applyRestoredStreak` shows a bought-back streak before the server refresh confirms it.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Challenge/DailyChallengeViewModel+Answering.swift`: `submitAnswer` and the outbox enqueue, the text and partner-choice edits, and `shuffle` (skip).
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Challenge/DailyChallengeViewModel+Drafts.swift`: the per-question drafts, compose kind, staged photo and voice media (`stagePhoto`, `stageVoice`), and the pick-up of drafts the outbox returned.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Challenge/DailyChallengeViewModel+Sending.swift`: the "saved, sending" state (`SendingAnswer`, `isSending`, `sendingPreview`, `refreshSendingState`), rebuilt from the pending operations.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Challenge/DailyChallengeViewModel+Chat.swift`: the chat and history view model factories, `prepareChat`, thread summaries, unread chats, and the partner-answer seen state (`hasNewFromPartner`, `uploadSeenPartnerAnswers`).
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Challenge/DailyChallengeNoticeLocalization.swift`: localized title/message mapping for `DailyChallengeViewModel.Notice`.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Models/DailyChallengeSnapshot.swift`: today's question (`todayQuestion`) and the open exchanges from earlier days. `questionsAwaitingAnswer` are the questions the partner answered and the user can still answer, `earlierQuestionsAwaitingAnswer` the ones from earlier days, which the cards list as locked rows. `homeCardState` is `noChallenge` before today's question exists, `active` until the user answers it, then `complete`.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Models/DailyChallengeModels.swift`: UI/domain models and answer-kind shape. Both partners answer the same question, so a question has no owner: `isAwaitingCurrentUserAnswer` is a question the partner answered and the user can still answer. `DailyChallengeQuestion` carries `earlierRoundCount`, `latestEarlierRoundLocalDate` and `hasSharedRound`. `hasRevealedPartnerAnswer` is per round (the new-answer marks use it) and `isChatAvailable` is per question (`hasRevealedPartnerAnswer || hasSharedRound`).
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Models/DailyChallengeRemoteRows.swift`: DTOs matching the `backend/convex/daily.ts` function results. Question rows carry `earlier_round_count`, `latest_earlier_round_local_date` and `chat_available` (see the question chat paragraph under `Chat/`).
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Data/DailyChallengeService.swift`: Convex calls for challenge/day/question/answer behavior.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Data/DailyChallengeDraftStore.swift`: local answer draft persistence.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Data/DailyChallengeSnapshotCache.swift`: local-first cold-launch cache. The live service writes the raw `DailyChallengeRemoteSnapshotRow` (keyed per user) on every successful snapshot load; `DailyChallengeViewModel.configure` seeds `snapshot`+`streak` from it via `DailyChallengeRemoteSnapshotRow.loadResult` before the network `reload()`, so returning users see real content (not placeholders) on the first frame. The centralized local privacy purger clears it on relationship access loss or user departure.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Data/DailyChallengeLiveFeed.swift`: live feed for today's challenge. It follows `daily:get_daily_challenge_snapshot` for the known couple day, or `get_today_daily_challenge_snapshot` when no day exists yet. Every today subscription (and every one-shot `loadTodaySnapshot`) uses a fresh random `p_day_key`, because the couple day ends at the westmost partner's midnight, not this phone's, so a per-local-date key could be served a stale result. Without a day the today subscription is renewed every half hour or at local midnight. Each result goes into the snapshot cache through `DailyChallengeLiveWriteClock` and posts `LocalStoreDomain.dailyChallenge`; `DailyChallengeViewModel` re-reads the cache and applies it. It never starts a day.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/Chat/Data/ThreadChatLiveFeed.swift`: live feed for `threads:get_conversation_threads` summaries (posts `LocalStoreDomain.threadChat`). An open chat follows `threads:get_thread_messages` through `ThreadChatServicing.liveMessageUpdates` and the question's days through `liveRoundUpdates` (see `Chat/` below), which merges each result into `FileThreadChatCache` and links server copies of still-sending messages to their operations (`ThreadChatMessage.claimingPending`).
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/DailyChallenge/Data/DailyChallengePendingOperationHandler.swift`: local-first retry handling for queued challenge writes. A submit the server refuses for good (`23514`, `23505`, `22023`) becomes a terminal failure and the answer goes back into the drafts (`DailySubmitAnswerOperationPayload.Content.restoredDraft`), which `DailyChallengeViewModel` picks up when the drain posts `.pendingOperations`. Upload errors and access errors (`42501`) keep retrying. `DailyChallengeLiveWriteClock.notePurge()` (called from the snapshot cache's `clearAll`) stops a response that was in flight during a privacy purge from writing the couple's content back.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/Chat/`: the one chat for every conversation. It has the page (`ThreadChatView`) and view model (`ThreadChatViewModel`), the thread/message RPC service (`ThreadChatService`), the owner-scoped local cache (`FileThreadChatCache`), optimistic merge rules, and pending-operation handlers for lazy thread creation and sends. The chat copies Tidex's friends chat (`~/repo/Tidex/ios/TidexApp/Features/Friends/Messages/`) on the same `hkarlsen06/Chat` fork commit: `.animateMessageUpdates(false)`, a custom composer through `inputViewBuilder`, a zero-height `betweenListAndInputViewBuilder`, day dividers, and the long-press menu (`Thread/ThreadChatMenuAction`). `Thread/ThreadChatProjection` builds ExyteChat's messages and the row models once per data change and folds everything a row draws into `triggerRedraw`, so ExyteChat redraws only rows that changed. `Thread/ThreadChatMessageRow` draws Paeonia's question (logo avatar), the answers, and messages, with grouped bubble corners, quotes above replies, voice messages, and a Sending / Sent / Not sent line. `Thread/ThreadChatGestures` and `Thread/ThreadChatGestureBridges` are the Tidex swipe gestures: swipe a bubble toward the middle to reply, swipe left to show times, swipe right to go back. Answers take the reply swipe too. A reply carries a `ThreadChatItemID` (a message or an answer), sent as `p_reply_to_message_id` or `p_reply_to_answer_id`, and the first message of a chat can already reply to an answer. Reactions, like Tidex's: long-pressing a sent message or a readable answer, the user's own included, shows ExyteChat's reaction row (`ReactionPalette`: the person's recent picks first, filled up with the usual six, kept in `UserDefaults` and cleared on sign-out, plus emoji search), and choosing the reaction already there takes it back. Each person has one reaction per item, keyed by `ThreadChatItemID` (a message or an answer, the same id a reply uses). `ThreadChatViewModel+Reactions` keeps the change in its own `ReactionEditor` (see Reactions) and saves it straight through `reactions:set_reaction`, not the send queue, and a refusal puts the earlier reaction back with a banner. Message reactions arrive on each message row (`ThreadChatMessage.reactions`) and answer reactions with the rounds (`DailyQuestionChatRoundsRow.reactions`). `ReactionBadge` draws them on the bubble's top corner. `Thread/ThreadChatComposer` is the text field and reply banner, and its microphone records with `DailyVoiceRecorder` (the voice-answer recorder). Replies send `p_reply_to_message_id` to `threads:send_thread_message`, and the long-press menu reports a partner message as a `thread_message` target. Edit: for 5 minutes after sending, the menu offers Edit on your own text messages and photo captions, never on answers or voice messages. The composer shows `Thread/ThreadChatEditBanner`, holds the earlier draft until the edit ends, and saves straight through `threads:edit_thread_message` instead of the send queue. The server checks the sender and the 5-minute window and sets `edited_at`, and the live `get_thread_messages` query brings the new text to both phones. The row adds Edited to the small line under the bubble, next to Sent or Read. Delete: the long-press menu offers Delete on any message of your own (text, photos, voice) with no time limit, behind a confirmation dialog (`Thread/ThreadChatDeleteConfirmation`). `threads:delete_thread_message` keeps the row as a tombstone (`deleted_at` set, text and reactions removed, media queued for storage deletion), and `get_thread_messages` returns it with no text, media or reactions. Both phones draw `Thread/ThreadChatDeletedNote` ("You deleted this message") in its place, and a reply that quoted it says the message was deleted. Delete works offline like a send: `ThreadChatService+Deleting` queues a `delete_thread_message` operation (`DeleteChatMessageOperationHandler`), shows the tombstone at once, and re-applies pending deletes to every server result (`withDeletions`) so a late live result cannot bring the message back. A message that was never tried is removed from the send queue with `PendingSyncOperationPersisting.discardUnsent` and never sent. `discardUnsent` refuses a write that was tried before, because the server may have it when the answer got lost. For such a message `ThreadChatService+Deleting` hides it and queues a `delete_thread_message` whose payload targets the send (`DeleteChatMessagePayload.Target.sentBy`). `DeleteChatMessageOperationHandler` retries until that send finishes, then deletes the message id the server made, or does nothing when the send never reached the server. A message that is sending right now, or whose chat the next queued message waits for, raises `stillSending` and a banner. The silent `thread_message_deleted` push clears the alert and calls `ThreadChatServicing.applyRemoteDeletion`, which marks the message deleted in the cached history of any chat. Opening a chat reads the older pages again as far back as the cache goes (`ThreadChatService+Revalidating`, at most `revalidationPageLimit` pages, then it drops the older cached messages), so a message deleted while the phone was away loses its text and files. Photos and recordings of a deleted message leave `DailyAnswerMediaImageService` (`evict`) on both phones. Typing dots and read receipts use Tidex's timings in `ThreadChatPresenceModel`: a typing heartbeat at most every 2.5 s through `chatPresence:set_chat_typing`, a stop after 4 s idle or on send, and the partner's dots hidden 2 s after their typing ends. The server keeps typing in the presence component and ends it 4 s after the last heartbeat, such as when the app was killed. `chatPresence:get_chat_presence` is one live query for whether the partner is typing and their read mark. Typing is keyed by the couple and the question, so it works before the thread exists and is shared by every day of the question. Reading is marked with `chatPresence:mark_thread_read` 200 ms after the newest message shows while the chat is on screen. The projection puts Read on your newest message the partner has read through and Sent on your newest message. Photos: up to 4 per message from the composer's drawer (`Thread/ThreadChatComposerAttachments`), compressed when picked with `ImageCompressor`, uploaded with the `thread_media` purpose under the message's id, and shown at fixed sizes by `Thread/ThreadChatPhotoViews`. Tapping one opens every chat photo in `PaeoniaImageViewer`. `threads:get_thread_messages` returns one page, `{ messages, has_more }`, newest 50 first and older pages through `p_before_created_at` and `p_before_message_id`. The open chat follows the newest page, and `ExyteChat`'s `enableLoadMoreOlderMessages` calls `ThreadChatViewModel.loadOlderMessages` when the person scrolls up. Each message carries `media` with each attachment's type and size, and `get_conversation_threads` returns `last_message_media_type` for the preview. `DailyChatRounds.openingScroll` decides where the page opens: at the newest message when someone has written since the newest day began, otherwise at the start of the day the user came from (the question's newest day for a chat opened from a message or the Home card). Callers await `DailyChallengeViewModel.prepareChat(for:focusesNewest:)` before pushing it, and the Questions screen hides the tab bar before the push. `PaeoniaImageMemoryCache` keeps decoded avatars and answer photos in memory so rebuilt rows don't flash placeholders. The privacy cleanup empties it with the disk caches. Until some day of the question has both answers revealed the page shows the question and answers and who it waits for, with no composer. Voice messages: the queued operation carries the .m4a bytes, and the send handlers upload them through `LiveDailyAnswerMediaUploadService` with the `thread_message_media` parent before sending. The server gives the message the id the upload was reserved under, which is how the server copy is matched to the pending one. Unread state is kept only on this phone. The cache stores when the user last saw each thread (`readThroughByThreadID`), and an open chat moves that mark forward. A chat is unread when its newest message is from the partner and is newer than the mark (`ThreadChatSummary.isUnread`). `DailyChallengeViewModel.unreadChats` drives the Home `HomeUnreadChatsCard` and the unread border on question rows. The Questions tab badge comes from the server (`AppBadges`). A fresh install shows every chat whose last message is from the partner as unread. **One chat per question.** A couple has one chat per question (`daily_question_threads` is keyed by couple and `question_id`, not by question version or instance), and every message, summary, cache entry, pending operation, typing room and push deep link (`paeonia://daily/chat?questionId=`) is keyed by the question id. ## Look-back card

- `backend/convex/lookBack.ts` and `lib/lookBack.ts`: `lookBack:get_look_back` returns at most one old item for the viewer's couple, or null. The query reads no clock. The app passes `p_now`, its time rounded down to 15 minutes, and the couple-local date comes from that time and the partners' zones (`resolveCoupleDayAnchorAt`). The result is `{ item, ends_at }`, where `ends_at` is when that couple day ends. Candidates are question rounds both partners answered (`getDailyAnswerDetails` applies the reveal, moderation and deleted rules), visible widget drawings, and visible memories, all at least 7 days old by content date (question: `couple_days.local_date`, drawing: `created_at` in the anchor zone, memory: `memory_date`). Reads are bounded: the newest 60 per source before the cutoff, plus indexed lookups of the same month and day in each of the last 5 years. The question pool is built from both partners' newest answers and keeps only rounds both answered, so it does not depend on who asks. Items from that month and day in an earlier year win. The pick starts at an FNV hash of couple id and date and walks the sorted candidates until one loads, so both partners get the same item all day. Result: `kind`, `content_date`, `on_this_day`, and one of `question` (instance id, both short prompts, `own_answer` and `partner_answer` with a 200-character text excerpt, partner pick, photo and voice flags), `drawing` (revision id, media asset id, author, canvas side) or `memory` (id, title, first visible photo asset id). Media always goes through the existing signed-URL services. Tests in `backend/convex/lookBack.test.ts`.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/LookBack/`: `Models/LookBackItem.swift` (the result, plus `destination`), `Models/LookBackItem+Display.swift` (what an answer line shows), `Data/LookBackService.swift` (one-shot read), `Data/LookBackCache.swift` (per-user file cache with the couple id, cleared by the privacy purge in `LiveLocalPrivateContentPurger`), `LookBackViewModel.swift`, `HomeLookBackCard.swift` and `LookBackContent.swift` (the card), and `LookBackRefresh.swift` (the Home modifier). ## Account Lifecycle And Local Privacy

- `ios/PaeoniaApp/App/RootViewModel.swift` (with its `RootViewModel+*.swift` extensions) and `ios/PaeoniaApp/Services/Auth/`: root-owned sign-out/deletion lifecycle, paired profile updates, and access re-resolution. Sign-out first calls `PaeoniaSyncService.flushPendingWrites` (at most 4 seconds) so waiting writes go out while the account can still send, then leaves Clerk and purges whatever is still unsent. `AuthNetworkDeadline` caps the network part of sign-in. Clerk sign-in plus the first profile load get 30 seconds (Google's sheet is not counted), and the profile load at launch gets 15 seconds before the cached profile opens the app. `AppleSignInService` ends an earlier Apple attempt that never reported back as cancelled when a new one starts, or when its caller is cancelled, so a new attempt always starts. Account deletion obtains a fresh Apple authorization code when possible. Cancelling that Apple sheet keeps the account; any other Apple failure still deletes and shows the manual-revocation step.
- `backend/convex/accountDeletion.ts` and `backend/convex/lib/accountDeletion.ts`: atomic Paeonia-data deletion boundary, relationship ending, device/location/notification cleanup, profile anonymization, retryable deletion jobs drained by a cron, and operator retry functions. `identity:delete_account` is the app-callable action.
- `backend/convex/dataExport.ts`, `dataExportBuild.ts` and `lib/dataExport*.ts`: "Download my data" (GDPR Art. 15 and 20). `lib/dataExportRegistry.ts` lists every table and how it is exported, and `lib/dataExportRegistry.test.ts` fails when a table in `schema.ts` is missing. `request_data_export` queues a job, the Node actions in `dataExportBuild.ts` build zip files (README, JSON per table, the person's original media files), the person gets a push, and signed `GET /data-export` links serve the zips for three days. Design and the scope rule for shared content: `docs/privacy-and-data-export.md`.
- `backend/convex/lib/accountDeletionProviders.ts`: outbound calls for account deletion, Clerk user deletion and Sign in with Apple token revocation. Required environment variables are listed in `backend/AGENTS.md`.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Storage/Privacy/`: centralized, idempotent owner-scoped privacy purge. Temporary entitlement loss hides widget/media/location derivatives while preserving drafts and pending work; permanent relationship loss purges relationship content; sign-out/account deletion remove all owner SwiftData rows, drafts, caches, widget/App Group files, invite credentials, and persisted relationship identifiers.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Services/Access/AccessLiveFeed.swift` and `RelationshipEventsLiveFeed.swift`: live subscriptions to `access:get_access_snapshot` and `relationship:get_relationship_sync_events`. The access feed writes the snapshot store (skipping identical snapshots), and `RootViewModel` observes `LocalStoreChanges` for `.access` and re-routes: partner accepts an invite, partner leaves or blocks, entitlement changes, and partner name, photo, or start date edits. The events feed saves events, applies them, and persists its cursor only after the apply succeeds, then resubscribes from the new cursor. Root's own purge pauses every feed and runs quiesced, so it also removes anything a stale couple subscription wrote in the meantime.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Services/Sync/Streams/RelationshipSyncEventApplier.swift`: maps backend `local_purge_scope` hints to temporary-hide or permanent-purge behavior, treating relationship-end `pending_uploads: review` as permanent for MVP because no recovery UI exists. Purge-driving events require the sync session's current `relationshipCoupleID`; an old relationship event cannot erase a newly paired couple's local state, and unknown relationship identity favors no data loss until Root resolves the access snapshot.
- `ios/PaeoniaAppTests/Sync/LocalPrivacyPurgeTests.swift`, `PaeoniaSyncServiceTests.swift`, `RelationshipEventsLiveFeedTests.swift`, and `AccessLiveFeedTests.swift`: owner isolation, idempotence, temporary-versus-permanent retention, coordinator wiring, and cross-couple regression coverage.

Days together (small, medium) shows the count and the progress to the next milestone. - `ios/PaeoniaApp/Features/WidgetDrawing/Canvas/DrawingFloodFill.swift`: the paint bucket. ## Sync, Access, And Location

- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Services/Sync/PaeoniaSyncService.swift` and `PaeoniaSyncServiceDefaults.swift`: orchestration for local-first sync work.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Services/Sync/SyncClientOperation.swift` and `SyncTypes.swift`: queued operation envelope and shared sync types.
- `ios/PaeoniaApp/Services/Sync/Streams/`: the pending operation drain (the offline write outbox) and the relationship event applier. Remote reads no longer poll. Each sync run sends every waiting write, oldest first and at most once per run, including writes still waiting out their retry delay. The delay (1 minute doubling to 5) only sets when the retry timer fires next. A write this build has no handler for stays queued without blocking the writes behind it. - `ios/PaeoniaApp/Services/Location/`: foreground location capture, gateway, models, and pending operation handling. Concurrent capture requests share one fix. While the app is open, a live feed reports 250 m moves to `LocationMapViewModel.submitObservedLocation`, which applies the same throttle as app open. While the app is closed, it uses the significant-change service, but only when the "Share while Paeonia is closed" choice is on and the person allowed Always. The phone reports the choice and its iOS location access to the server (`location:report_location_device_state`) when either changes and once per launch. After a new sign-in or relationship it takes the choice the server kept, unless the person sets the switch first. Turning sharing on from the map card or the location primer turns that choice on and asks iOS for Always right away (`offerUpdatesWhileClosed`, called from `RootView.shareLocation`). The Settings switch changes it later. `BackgroundLocationUploader` then sends the position directly, since the outbox only runs while the app is open. `PaeoniaAppDelegate` re-arms it at every launch, `RootView+System.swift` feeds it identity, sharing, pause, and scene phase, and the privacy purge resets it.
- Pausing location sharing: a pause is a sharing choice with an end time, not an off switch. Sharing stays on, nothing is sent, and the server ends the pause by itself.
  - Backend: `location_sharing_preferences.paused_until` (index `by_paused_until`). `location:update_location_sharing_preference` takes `p_paused_until` (only with `p_is_enabled: true`, at most 48 hours ahead, a time already past is ignored) and keeps the stored location, which only turning sharing off deletes. `update_latest_partner_location` refuses with `42501 location sharing is paused` until the time, and the first update after the time ends the pause itself. The `resume-paused-location-sharing` cron (every minute, `location:resume_paused_location_sharing`) clears expired pauses, refreshes the person's own Lock Screen, and for a phone with updates while closed on sends the silent `location_pause_ended` push so it shares where it is now. `lib/locationPause.ts` has `isSharingNow`, which reads the stored pause and not the clock. The "partner shares" check for location pushes uses it, and the Lock Screen distance uses it for the viewer only, so a paused partner's last distance stays. Tests: `backend/convex/location.test.ts` ("pausing location sharing") and `widget.test.ts` ("distance").
  - What the partner sees: nothing about the pause. `get_partner_location_visibility` answers `viewer_paused` (with `viewer_paused_until`) only to the person who paused, and they see no position. Their partner gets `visible` with the last stored position, so the map shows where the person last was and how old it is, as for a phone that has not sent a new one. The Lock Screen distance works the same way. The pause copy tells the person who pauses that their partner is not told.
  - Phone: `Services/Location/LocationPauseOption.swift` turns "1 hour", "4 hours" and "until morning" (the next 08:00 on the phone's clock at the tap, at least an hour ahead, daylight saving aware) into an end time and formats it. `LocationMapViewModel+Pause.swift` (`pauseSharing`, `resumeSharing`) writes the choice locally, queues it in the sharing operation (`LocationSharingOperationPayload.pausedUntil`), drops the own location and queued uploads, and tracks the end on a timer (`trackPause`). The unconfirmed-choice ledger holds `ViewerSharingChoice` (on or off plus the pause end), so a stale server answer cannot undo a pause or a resume. `BackgroundLocationUploader` sends nothing while paused (`.paused`), `LocationUpdatePlan` stops the open-app feed, and significant-change monitoring keeps running so iOS can still relaunch the app. `PaeoniaAppDelegate` answers `location_pause_ended` with `LocationUpdateController.shareAfterPauseEnded()`. UI: `Features/Location/Pause/` (`LocationPauseChoices`, `LocationPauseMenu` in the ready map's top corner), the pause rows in `LocationSettingsView`, and the `sharingPaused` map card.
