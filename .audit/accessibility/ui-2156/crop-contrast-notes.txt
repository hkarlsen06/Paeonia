Method and limits:
The script draws each profile-tagged PNG into an sRGB bitmap before linearizing its channels with WCAG relative luminance weights. It samples a glyph core only when every channel in its 3 by 3 neighborhood differs from the center by at most 1. This excludes thin antialias edges. It compares that core with the nearest flat dark background pixel in four directions, searching 2 to 80 physical pixels away. The CSV records the worst pair and its coordinates in the converted bitmap.

These exact crops contain light text and symbols on dark plum backgrounds. The diagnostic separates them at relative luminance 0.1. It is not suitable for an arbitrary screenshot, dark text, overlapping pictures, gradients without a nearby background sample, or thin glyphs with no flat interior. Dark foreground content could be mistaken for background. These crops were individually identified before measuring: one secondary-text paragraph, a native tab bar with white and pink labels/symbols, and the light Back label/chevron. No other dark text occurs in those crops. Lowering the initial glyph threshold from 0.3 to 0.1 produced identical sample counts and ratios; zero flat cores occur in 0.1 through 0.3. The method therefore does not omit medium-gray cores in these particular crops. It does not establish that every glyph or all antialias pixels were sampled.

- Paywall paragraph: 65,488 core/background pairs, minimum 8.174:1. Worst sRGB pair CBB9C3 / 42152E.
- Native tab bar labels and icons: 11,030 pairs, minimum 10.664:1. Worst pair FFA2D2 / 1A0310. This does not measure the tab selection boundary or establish non-color state differentiation.
- Sign-in Back label/chevron: 16,887 pairs, minimum 7.377:1. Worst pair CEBBC6 / 4F1D38.

The sampled text/icon colors exceed 4.5:1 in these crops. This is evidence for the individual issue review, pending reconciliation with the richer 2217 issue-node details. It does not justify ignoring an audit type, passing another state, or claiming an App Store label.
