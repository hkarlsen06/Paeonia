Paeonia is a private app for the two of you. Answer the same question each day, send a drawing to your partner's Lock Screen, and play games at your own pace.

One question a day
You both get the same question. Your partner's answer stays hidden until you've answered too.

Drawings on their screen
Draw something, and it shows up in the widgets on your partner's Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Games made for two
Ludo, Four in a Row, Mind Meld, Draw & Guess, Story Chain, Guess my spot and more. Play live when you're both here, or take your turn and let your partner reply when they can.

Your story together
Save memories with photos, count down to your next visit, and see your next anniversary coming.

Where your partner is, if you want
Share your latest location with your partner. Paeonia keeps no location history, and you can pause sharing at any time.

Just for the two of you
Nothing you share is public. Only your partner sees it. Paeonia has no ads, and it only counts how you use the app if you say yes.

One subscription for both of you
Paeonia needs an active subscription. One of you subscribes, and the other joins with the invite code at no extra cost. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in your Apple Account settings. Restoring a lost streak is an optional extra purchase.
