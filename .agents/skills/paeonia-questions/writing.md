# Writing Paeonia questions

These rules apply to every question Paeonia writes: daily questions, topic questions, game topics and story openings.

## Voice

Paeonia asks the question, not the partner. "You" is the signed-in person. Refer to the partner as "your partner" or "them", and to the couple as "the two of you" or "your relationship". Leave out "I", "me", "my", "we" and "our" wherever they could read as the partner speaking.

- Good: "What is one memory you have of them that you keep thinking of?"
- Bad: "What is one memory you have of me that you keep thinking of?"

In Norwegian the same applies to "jeg", "meg", "min", "vi" and "vår". Prefer "partneren din" when "dem" would feel vague.

- Good: "Hva er ett minne om partneren din som du stadig tenker på?"
- Bad: "Hva er ett minne om meg som du stadig tenker på?"

## A good question

Paeonia is small daily rituals that make distance feel less passive. The tone is private, calm and warm. Romantic is fine; cheesy, needy or manipulative is not.

A good question:

- takes 1-3 minutes and asks one clear thing
- gives one concrete handle, such as a moment, object, message, place, sound, plan or timeframe
- works asynchronously across time zones, with neither partner needing to be awake at the same time
- is understandable to an ordinary 16-year-old and safe to answer on a busy day

Prefer "What TV series reminds you the most of the future you imagine with your partner?" over "Record a voice note of something your partner would want to hear." The first gives a reference point. The second makes the person invent the context, the content and the reason before they can start.

A question stays clear of:

- guilt, pressure, jealousy, tests and scorekeeping
- assumptions about gender, marriage, living together, sex, religion, holidays or relationship stage
- sensitive disclosures, diagnosing the relationship, or forcing a conflict talk
- private content in the short prompt, which shows in pushes and compact cards

## Full and short prompt

The full prompt is a complete, direct question or instruction, shown where the person answers. The short prompt is a calm summary for cards and lists. It must not read like a notification from the partner.

| Full prompt | Short prompt |
| --- | --- |
| What is one memory you have of them that you keep thinking of? | A memory that stayed |
| What is one small thing about your partner you appreciated today? | A small appreciation |
| What could help your partner feel close to you this week? | A way to feel close |

## Norwegian

Localize the meaning, not the word order. Bokmål should sound like a normal person wrote it, not like translated database text. Keep slang out of translations.

## Topics

Topic questions are evergreen. They ask who someone is, what they have lived and what they hope for, so they avoid "today" and "this week".

## Flirt

Flirt is the one topic where questions may be flirty and suggestive. Every couple sees it, so it stays within the 16+ App Store rating: suggestive, never explicit.

Flirt questions may ask about attraction, what someone likes about how their partner looks or dresses, kissing and being held, flirting, romantic evenings, and messages they would like to get. Each one must:

- work for a couple who isn't having sex, and for a couple who is apart, so being together is a what-if ("If your partner were next to you right now")
- be answerable in a few playful words, or skippable without a second thought
- name the theme in the short prompt, never the detail

Flirt questions never mention sex acts, nudity or anything explicit, never ask for a detailed fantasy, never ask how often, how good or with whom, and never ask about past partners. They never use the `photo` answer kind, so no prompt invites a photo of a body. Flirt content never appears in pushes, widgets, Look Back or Home.
